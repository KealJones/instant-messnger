Instant Mess'nger

Actually instant. Nothing saved. You had to be there.

How this works
  • Everyone in the room types into one shared line. Letters land in the order they arrive.
  • Each letter you type earns you one backspace. A backspace crosses out the newest letter in the line, whoever typed it.
  • After 5 seconds of nobody typing, the line locks. The next keystroke wipes it and starts a new one. Backspaces reset.
  • Nothing is stored. If you weren't here, you missed it.
